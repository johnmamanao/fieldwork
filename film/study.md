# Study

Three launch films from Seenry's motion library (category "Launch videos", query "saas"). Measured with ffmpeg (scene cuts at threshold 0.3, EBU R128 loudness) and read from contact sheets at one frame per 2 s. I could not listen to them, so sound notes are measurement only.

## Linear Loops — Linear (seenry 83a4fb4bdceeeb3426d6b6c67d0f50b0, 40.5 s, 1920×1080)
- **Structure:** opens straight on the idea (orbits labelled with workflow steps: "Issue enters triage", "Find bug labels", "Start coding session"), resolves at ~28 s to the Loops mark, "Now available in Linear", then the Linear wordmark; ~10 s of black tail.
- **Pacing:** 0 hard cuts. One continuous camera through 3D orbits; steps light up in sequence.
- **Type and picture:** mono uppercase labels small on the motion; display type only on the end card. Stylised, not product UI.
- **Sound:** −18.0 LUFS integrated: quiet, restrained.
- **Take:** continuity instead of cuts; the steps of a loop lighting up in order (our chain: log → approve → invoice → paid → see); a calm end card.
- **Reject:** stylised abstraction instead of the product; the long black tail.

## tinycrm launch — Dmytro Virych (seenry ab5c58106b1c65b943b9a938bbd98650, 32.1 s, 1920×1080)
- **Structure:** problem ("Your customers are everywhere.") → turn ("There's a better way.") → proof (real table "Every customer in one table.", "Merged by email.", "Two lines of code.", "That's the whole setup.", "Live in seconds.") → "All your products. One database." → wordmark.
- **Pacing:** 0 hard cuts; each sentence holds ~2.5–3 s, one sentence per beat, the UI morphs between beats.
- **Type and picture:** one centred sentence (~40px at 1080p) under one real UI element; dark ground; a founder webcam bubble bottom-left.
- **Sound:** −9.4 LUFS: much louder than the −14 target.
- **Take:** the grammar: one real UI piece + one short sentence per beat, the UI large enough to read, short declarative lines.
- **Reject:** the webcam bubble; UI shown too small for a phone feed; the hot master.

## Autostep — Y Combinator (seenry 1e186eee3e4f3ff3d1faf9c372124b00, 65.0 s, 1920×1080)
- **Structure:** founder to camera → real product recordings with cursor → founder → stock office crowd → product → AI-looking stock scenes (handshake, paper stacks) → founder.
- **Pacing:** 13 cuts, median shot 3.2 s (1.2–14.4 s).
- **Type and picture:** real UI with a visible cursor and a figure ($847,219) to anchor the screen; persistent logo bug.
- **Sound:** −24.9 LUFS, voice-led.
- **Take:** real UI with one figure as the anchor of each screen; a 3 s shot rhythm.
- **Reject:** stock and generated scenes, the talking head, the logo bug.

## Direction
- **60 s, 120 BPM, 30 bars**, nine beats. Light warm ground for product shots, vermilion hours-grid for the hook, ink for the end card.
- **Grammar (from tinycrm):** each beat = one real capture, pushed in on the part that matters, plus one sentence (52px Manrope, centred under the shot). Camera moves continuously (slow push / pan); scenes change on bar lines with a short cross-move, no wipes.
- **Signature moment:** the lock. Leo's approved entries shown with the "Invoiced" lock badge, with the line "Billed once. Then locked."
- **Sound:** an original synthesised score at 120 BPM: muted pulse for the hook, the beat drops when the product appears (bar 3), a breakdown before the turn (bar 11), the fullest bar on the lock, resolving on one chord for the end card. Effects only on real events: the timer start, the approval, the lock, the paid balance, the end card. Master at −14 LUFS.
- **Seen once and unverified:** whether the tinycrm sentence timing follows its music (no cuts to measure against).
