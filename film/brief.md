# Fieldwork product film: brief

**Claim (what the viewer repeats):** In Fieldwork, an approved hour is billed once, and a payment closes it.

**Proof (what the viewer sees):** one real chain in the seeded demo studio (Larkspur & Co.):
Leo Martins starts a timer on *Winter campaign microsite* (HAL-02) from the top bar → submits his week → Avery Brooks approves it → Avery drafts an invoice for Halden Outdoor Co. / HAL-02, adds Leo's 9 approved entries (13.5 h, $2,025.00) and issues it as LK-0029 → Leo's rows show the Invoiced lock → a settled bank transfer takes LK-0029 to $0.00 → Analytics reads the same records (billable hours by day, as a skyline). The 0:08–0:28 chain is one continuous screen recording of the running app (rec.mjs); the demo data was restored afterwards.

**Turn (signature shot):** the time entries flip to the lock badge "Invoiced": the same hours cannot be billed again.

**Audience:** owners, finance leads and project managers at services studios who reconcile time, invoices and payments by hand.

**Where it plays:** the landing page and social (X, LinkedIn). 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Must read on a phone in the feed.

**Length:** 60 s (30 bars at 120 BPM).

**Voice:** none. One short on-screen sentence per beat (tinycrm grammar). Music and a small effects kit carry the rhythm.

**Fixed:**
- Brand: vermilion #f0512a / #b4351a, ink #121214, warm light #f5f4f2, Manrope + Geist Mono, notched pixel buttons, the hours-grid and ASCII wordmark motifs.
