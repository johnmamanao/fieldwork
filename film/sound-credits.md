# Sound credits

All sound in this film was composed and synthesised for it in code by `synth.py` (numpy only). No samples, libraries, recordings or generation services were used, so there is nothing to license or attribute.

| File | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| media/music.wav | 60 s score, 120 BPM, A minor (Am–F–C–G) | `synth.py`: sine/harmonic pads, plucked arpeggio, eighth-note bass, synthesised kick, hats and clap; sections follow the cut |
| media/sfx/click.wav | the timer start and the approval | filtered noise transient plus a short 1.8 kHz tone |
| media/sfx/tick.wav | the live cell, Leo's invoice line, the analytics ring and the recap beats | 2.2 kHz blip |
| media/sfx/lock.wav | each "Invoiced" lock at the turn | low body thump, latch transient and a short metallic ring |
| media/sfx/chime.wav | the $0.00 balance and the end card | two bell partials, A5 and E6 |

No voice.
