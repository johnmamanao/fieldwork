# Fieldwork sign-in

Audience: people at a services studio (owners, finance, project managers, staff) returning to Fieldwork to log time, approve weeks, send invoices and record payments. Also evaluators arriving from the landing page's "Explore the demo" link, who want to try a role without creating an account.

The one job: get a returning person signed in fast; let an evaluator enter a demo role in one click.

Real content (fixed, all must remain):
- Fieldwork logo and wordmark linking to the landing page
- Link to create a workspace for new studios
- Email field, password field with show/hide, "Forgot password?" link
- Sign in button with busy state; inline field errors, a form-level error and a shake on rejection
- Notices from query params: session expired, email verified, password reset, no active workspace
- Seven demo accounts from /demo-accounts (role, person, workspace), one-click sign in with a spinner on the chosen one; ?demo=1 scrolls to them
- Works from 320px to wide desktop; keyboard and screen-reader complete

Fixed brand: Manrope (UI, weights 400-600), Geist Mono (figures only), ink #121214, accent #f0512a used sparingly, warm-neutral greys from the app tokens, the F-arc logo. The landing page is calm, quiet-premium, solo-pos-like: lots of space, few lines.

Intent: a sign-in that feels like opening the studio's own ledger, not a SaaS template with a marketing panel.
