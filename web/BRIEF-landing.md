# Fieldwork landing page

Audience: owners, finance leads and project managers at services studios (agencies, design and engineering studios) who bill by the hour and reconcile time, invoices and payments by hand.

The one job: show that Fieldwork turns approved time into invoices exactly once and closes them with payments, then get the visitor into the demo or a new workspace.

Real content (all present):
- Floating dark nav: logo, Product, Tour, Analytics, Access, Sign in, Start free
- Hero: claim, lead, Create a workspace and Explore the demo, the real Overview screen with two live callouts
- Module strip: Time, Approvals, Invoices, Payments, Analytics, Chat
- [00] How the records connect: five steps and the isometric record stack, assembled while pinned
- [01] Watch it work: five real screens with step pills and a module dock
- [02] Across the studio: five cards with real screens
- [03] Analytics: three cards (skyline, revenue, delivery report)
- [04] Access: deny by default, audit log, role switcher with what each role sees
- [05] Get started: demo roles with photos, create a workspace
- Footer with link trees and a pixel wordmark

Fixed brand: Manrope 500 for headlines, Geist Mono uppercase for labels and buttons, ink #121214, vermilion #f0512a as the only hue, the F-arc logo. All screenshots are the real seeded demo studio (Larkspur & Co.).
