# Fieldwork design record

## Brand guidelines

Idea: the sign-in is the studio's front door. Returning people get one quiet card; evaluators see the demo studio's people and what each role opens, so choosing a demo account is choosing a view of the studio. It comes from the claim that one set of approved records feeds every role's view.
Bar: Mercury (card on a tinted ground, quiet 400-500 title, a separate zone for the second path), Linear (sparseness, one column of decisions), Stripe (the other path gets its own place). Harvest confirmed the time-tracking category keeps sign-in to one centered column.
Type: Manrope throughout; titles 500, labels 500, roles 600; Geist Mono only for aligned figures (none on auth screens). Weights 400, 500, 600.
Palette: ground #f4f4f5 (--color-bg), surface #ffffff, ink #121214 (primary action, logo tile), accent #f0512a (logo dot and focus ring only), text greys from tokens.
Imagery: none on auth screens. The material is the seeded workspace's people and permissions.
Owned detail: the F-arc logo, and the roster line "Or try the demo as someone at Larkspur & Co.".
Rules:
- Do keep sign-in to one card; never a marketing side panel, slogan or mockup on auth screens.
- Do separate rows by space and a hover surface; never by rules.
- Do use the ink button as the only filled action on a screen.
- Don't use uppercase letter-spaced labels or "or" dividers.
- Don't show developer wording (localhost, Mailpit) anywhere in the UI.

## Brand guidelines: landing page (2026-11-06)

- Idea: the billing week taken apart into its records (time entry, approval, invoice, payment, overview), worn in a dithered-pixel language.
- Type: Manrope 500 for headlines (64/70, tracking -0.028em; h2 44px), Manrope 500 body at 16 to 18px; Geist Mono 500 uppercase tracked 0.07em at 14 to 15px for section labels and buttons.
- Palette: ink #121214 (nav, dark bands, footer); warm light #f5f4f2; hero field vermilion #b4351a, with #e24a25, #f0512a, #ff7d55 and #ffc2ad pixels; accent #f0512a on buttons with ink text.
- Material: the hero and sign-in panel ground is an hours grid (`shared/hours-field.ts`): day cells lit by hours logged, with a walking "today" column; other pixel fields drawn in code (`shared/dither.ts`, Bayer 4x4) for the dark stages and the CTA haze; seams between sections (`shared/pixel-edge.ts`); the footer wordmark as ASCII art (ramp ` .:-=+*#%`, dense at the top of each letter), each character a particle that moves away from the pointer (`shared/particle-word.ts`); every screenshot is the real seeded demo studio (public/landing/tour-*.jpg, shot-*.jpg).
- Owned detail: the isometric record stack (`shared/iso-records.ts`) carries one example through all five records.
- Motion: hero settles in once (600ms, 80ms stagger); pixel fields drift at about 12fps only while on screen; the streak pulses; the wordmark scans and answers the pointer; section labels unscramble once; all of it stops under reduced motion.
- Rules: one hue (vermilion) only; mono only for labels and buttons; no text under 14px on desktop; real product screens only; seams only between light and dark sections.
