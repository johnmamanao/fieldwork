# Brief — Fieldwork PSA

Surface: app

Audience: agency owners, project managers, finance leads and delivery staff at a 10–60 person services studio.

The one job: show, from approved records, whether the studio is delivering on time, using its people well, and getting paid, then let each role act on it (approve time, issue an invoice, record a payment, move a task).

Real content: Larkspur & Co. demo workspace — 8 clients, 10 projects, 13 staff, six months of time, invoices, payments, chat.

Fixed: Laravel API + Angular SPA + Docker; modules from psa-complete-modules.md; grouped sidebar Overview, Projects, Time, Analytics, Reports, Chat, Clients, Invoices, Payments, Cards, Users, Staff, Notifications, Settings, Audit log. Light neutral canvas, restrained borders, rounded panels, strong heading hierarchy, warm chart accent (spec §21). Inspired by Divan Raj (@desdivj), structured/utility expression — interpretation, not his system.
